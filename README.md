**DOLCE** is a modern, cross-platform music streaming application built with **Flutter**, delivering a fast, elegant, and immersive listening experience. Inspired by the simplicity and intelligence of today's leading music platforms, DOLCE combines intuitive design with powerful features such as music discovery, seamless playback, personalized recommendations, playlist management, and favorites.

Built with a scalable architecture and clean development practices, the project showcases modern Flutter development, Firebase integration, efficient state management, and responsive UI/UX principles. DOLCE is designed as both a production-inspired application and a portfolio project, demonstrating the development of a complete, feature-rich music streaming platform.

### ✨ Why DOLCE?

Derived from the Italian musical term *dolce* ("sweetly"), the name reflects the app's mission—to provide a smooth, enjoyable, and delightful music experience for every listener.
